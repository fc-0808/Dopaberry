Before the first order

Y2KASEshop · Hinfung Eco-Tech Group Limited · United States

In short. Publish the product onto 4PX West LA, then ask 4PX which door is yours and which label they will apply.

  1. Upload the product in Seller Center.
  2. Assign its stock to the warehouse named 4PX West LA.
  3. Ask 4PX which code your account uses, USLAXA or USLAXC, and whether that building will apply a TikTok label or only ship on P6US03.
  4. If their door is not 8369 Milliken Ave, edit the pickup warehouse before any buyer can order.
  5. Choose the path before the product can be bought. Path A: leave TikTok Shipping off and ship on P6US03. Path B: switch the product to TikTok Shipping only after 4PX agrees to print that outside label.
  6. On Path A, type the platform as TikTok in 4PX, then paste the 4PX tracking number onto the TikTok order.
  7. Keep lookalike branded goods out. Pack a battery item in a rigid carton.

Changing the address later

The address can still be edited. The new address applies to new orders. An order that already has a label keeps the old address. TikTok will not move that order.

  • Seller Center path: Logistics, then Warehouse & shipping, then Warehouse settings. Use Edit on 4PX West LA.
  • You can keep up to 20 pickup warehouses. One of them is the default.
  • If Edit will not change the street, add the correct warehouse, make it the default, move the products onto it, then deactivate the old one.
  • The last remaining pickup warehouse cannot be deactivated. A deactivated warehouse cannot be turned back on.
  • TikTok checks the street against USPS. ZIP 91730 belongs with Rancho Cucamonga, not with Los Angeles or Commerce.

A return warehouse can be deleted once another one exists. USLAXB may be a different door from Milliken Avenue. Leave returns on TikTok’s Commerce dock until 4PX gives you that street.

Warehouse and Shipping Setup says the address can be updated. US Warehouse Management, 17 September 2026, covers the 20-warehouse limit and deactivation.
