Which 4PX building

Y2KASEshop · Hinfung Eco-Tech Group Limited · United States

In short. Phone cases go in a general warehouse (综合仓), on the West Coast. 4PX assigns you one building, not every West code on the list.

General or bulky

In the price card, column 仓库定位 uses two words.

  • General warehouse (综合仓) is for ordinary parcels, including a phone case.
  • Bulky warehouse (大件仓) is for large goods. A case does not get a cheaper storage rate there, and the TikTok product does not ship from those buildings.

Why West, and why only one of them

USLAXA and USLAXC are two different buildings that can ship P6US03. Seeing two codes does not mean your account receives both, and it does not mean the West is twice as capable. 4PX assigns one. Use the code written on your account.

East New Jersey 1 can also ship the TikTok product. Handling there is slightly cheaper: about 0.19 USD in and 0.29 USD out for a piece under 0.5 kg, against 0.21 USD in and 0.31 USD out on the West. West is still the first building because the cartons leave Shenzhen and enter the United States on the Pacific side. Stocking the only inventory in New Jersey means paying to cross the country before the first order can ship.
