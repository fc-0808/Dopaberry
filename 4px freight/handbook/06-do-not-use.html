What not to use

Y2KASEshop · Hinfung Eco-Tech Group Limited · United States

In short. Do not book the economy product, do not use a bulky warehouse, do not send returns to Houston, and do not send lookalike branded goods.

Economy product

P6US01, Economy Best Choice (菜鸟美国经济优配), quotes 2 to 6 working days. Its platform list does not include TikTok. A TikTok order booked on it can be routed to the wrong carrier.

Bulky warehouses

West Los Angeles 5 (USLAJA), East New Jersey 2 (USNJCB), and Savannah 1 (USSAVA) are bulky warehouses (大件仓). They are built for large pieces. A phone case belongs in a general warehouse (综合仓).

Houston as a return address

USHOUA can ship P6US03. Consumer returns there are marked no service (无服务). The warehouse destroys them and bills a disposal fee.

Economy list: Best Choice sheet, row 20, left column. Bulky codes: 出入库操作服务价卡, rows 72, 75, and 76. Houston returns: 退件服务价卡, row 8.

Goods 4PX will reject

The operating manual does not accept counterfeit goods, lookalike branded goods, medicine, liquids, powder, or other prohibited items. A plain case is fine. A case printed with Apple, Disney, or another brand’s artwork is not.

A battery inside a product must be 100 Wh or under, declared honestly, and packed to the carrier’s rule. From 12 July 2026, USPS can charge 50 USD for one parcel that breaks the battery rule. USPS wants a rigid carton, not a soft mailer. The battery mark and the shipping label go on the same face of the carton.

Operating manual, section 5.4, 来货限制及注意事项. Battery rule: pre-shipment notes, sheet 发货限制说明, row 92.
