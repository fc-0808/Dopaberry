How an order ships

Y2KASEshop · Hinfung Eco-Tech Group Limited · United States

In short. 4PX packs the box. TikTok does not come into the building and do the packing. The Milliken address only tells TikTok where the parcel starts.

The warehouse screen and the shipping screen show four different services. They are easy to mix up because they sit on the same menus.

1. 4PX West LA

Active This is your ship-from address. When a buyer orders, TikTok calculates distance from Rancho Cucamonga. The goods still have to be inside a 4PX building, picked, and packed by 4PX.

2. TikTok Shipping

Approved, not on a product yet Standard shipping is turned on. Express shipping is off. The sentence under it, “You ship the order, we’ll take care of the rest,” means:

  • You, through 4PX, pack the parcel.
  • TikTok buys the postage and picks a carrier.
  • The carriers on Standard shipping are UPS Ground Saver, USPS Ground Advantage, and FedEx Ground Home Delivery.

The green box in Seller Center says this starts only after three things happen: the product is published, its stock is assigned to 4PX West LA, and you switch that product to TikTok Shipping (Upgraded).

3. Collection by TikTok

Not enrolled This is the service where a TikTok courier drives to the warehouse and collects parcels that are already packed. Your screen says the warehouse is not enrolled. Nobody is scheduled to pick up at Milliken Avenue.

4. Fulfilled by TikTok

Not approved FBT is TikTok’s own warehouse network. You would send stock to TikTok, and TikTok would store it, pick it, pack it, and ship it. The application for this shop was not approved. The FBT link on the warehouse page is an advertisement, not a service you have. Do not send cartons to TikTok for FBT.
