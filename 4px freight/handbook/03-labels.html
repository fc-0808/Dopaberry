One order, one label

Y2KASEshop · Hinfung Eco-Tech Group Limited · United States

In short. Each order gets one shipping label. Either 4PX prints it, or TikTok prints it. Never both.

Path A. 4PX makes the label. TikTok does not.

Use this when 4PX ships with its own US service. TikTok never sends a label.

  1. A buyer orders on TikTok.
  2. In 4PX, create the shipment as Standard Best Choice (菜鸟美国标准优配), code P6US03.
  3. Type the sales platform as TikTok. The rate card uses that field to choose 4PX’s carrier.
  4. 4PX packs the goods, prints its own label, and hands the parcel to the carrier on that service.
  5. Copy that tracking number onto the TikTok order. The number on the box and the number in TikTok must be the same.
  6. Do not buy a label inside TikTok for that order.

On this path, 4PX is the warehouse and 4PX’s carrier delivers. TikTok only shows the buyer the tracking number you enter.

The service covers the whole United States, quotes 2 to 5 working days, and stops taking same-day orders at 14:00 local warehouse time. A phone case is inside the limit: longest side up to 182.88 cm, chargeable weight up to 22.5 kg.

When you publish the product, do not switch it to TikTok Shipping if you want this path. That switch tells TikTok to create its own label.

P6US03 is not in the warehouse price card. Open the US Best Choice rate card, sheet CN_US_Best Choice服务范围, cell D1. Cell C1 is P6US01. Row 20, the right-hand column, is the only column that lists TikTok.

Path B. TikTok makes the label. The warehouse prints it.

This is the path where TikTok’s only shipping job is the label and the carrier choice. TikTok does not mail a paper label to the building. The label appears in Seller Center. Someone at the warehouse prints it.

Use it only after 4PX says they will apply an outside label and give the box to UPS, USPS, or FedEx.

  1. Publish the product, put the stock on 4PX West LA, and switch that product to TikTok Shipping (Upgraded).
  2. A buyer orders.
  3. TikTok creates a label. The sender address is 8369 Milliken Ave. The carrier is UPS Ground Saver, USPS Ground Advantage, or FedEx Ground Home Delivery.
  4. 4PX prints that label and sticks it on the parcel. 4PX does not print a second, 4PX label.
  5. 4PX hands the parcel to the carrier named on the TikTok label. 4PX packs. That carrier delivers. 4PX is not the delivery company on this path.

Collection by TikTok is not enrolled, so a TikTok driver is not coming to collect it. The pickup is whatever handover 4PX already uses for UPS, USPS, or FedEx.

If the goods are not actually at 8369 Milliken Ave, do not use Path B. The label would name the wrong building.

Easy to miss

  • The stock number on 4PX West LA has to match the boxes that are really there. Zero stock means the product cannot sell. A number that is too high sells a box you do not have.
  • Each TikTok order shows a ship-by time. That clock is TikTok’s. 4PX’s own cutoff, 14:00 local time, is a different clock.
  • The tracking number in TikTok must be the number printed on the box. A 4PX number on a USPS label, or the reverse, makes the buyer’s tracking look stalled.
  • Your shipping settings already show a 7.19 USD flat fee on LIVE orders that weigh 1 to 5 lb. That fee is separate from the 4PX bill.
  • Returns still go to TikTok in Commerce and are destroyed, on both paths.
  • The phone saved on the warehouse, 6579992018, is from a 2022 notice. A carrier may call it. Replace it if 4PX gives you a current dock number.
